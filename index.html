About

A small studio that takes its time.

GibPlay Studios makes games for Apple devices, built with care and meant to be returned to, whether for a minute or a whole evening.

  • Made by handEverything you see in Bitz, from the cat to the seasons outside the window, is original pixel art drawn in code by the game itself.
  • Respect for playersBitz has no ads, no accounts and no tracking. Your cat lives on your device.
  • Built for iPhone and iPadNative games that feel at home on Apple devices, from the Home Screen to the bedside.
