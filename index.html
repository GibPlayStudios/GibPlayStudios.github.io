About the studio

Small games, made with care.

We like games you can return to for a minute or an evening, and that respect your time and your privacy.

Everything you see in Bitz, from the cat and the furniture to the seasons outside the window, is original pixel art drawn in code by the game itself, so the app, its widgets and its App Store artwork all share one hand-made look.

Bitz has no ads, no accounts and no tracking. Your cat lives on your device.
