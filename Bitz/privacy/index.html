Effective October 18, 2026 (Bitz 1.0, build 6 and later). Applies to the Bitz app and its widgets for iPhone and iPad, to the gibplay.com web pages, and to email sent to info@gibplay.com.

In short: the Bitz app has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and there is no developer server: the app sends nothing to us. We receive only emails you choose to send us, such as a Report. Your game is saved on your device. Purchases go through Apple's App Store, which processes them under Apple's terms and gives us sales reports, not who you are. With Nearby Friends on, nearby players receive your cat's look under a code that changes each session, but not your names; your cat's name, your display name and your cat's permanent code go only to a player you invite or whose invitation you accept, and to the cats in a visit you join. If you email us, your message reaches our Gmail mailbox through Cloudflare, and our replies are sent through Brevo; see Support email and these web pages.

Who we are

Bitz is developed and published on the App Store by Berkay Yilmaz, an individual developer, under the studio name GibPlay Studios. He decides how the personal data described here is handled (the data controller). Contact: info@gibplay.com.

On your device only

  • Your game: your cat (its name and look), your display name, rooms, stars, items, purchases already unlocked, memories, friends, visit memories, blocked cats and settings are saved in one file on your device, shared between the Bitz app and its widgets. Bitz never uploads it.
  • Steps (optional): only if you choose Connect Health, Bitz reads today's step count from Apple Health to celebrate your walking goal and give star rewards. The latest count and the rewards are kept in your save. Bitz never sends step data anywhere, never writes to Health and never uses it for advertising.
  • Battery state: read while Bitz is open, for the cozy charging scenes; the latest reading is kept in your save.
  • Photos (optional): Bitz can add a picture you choose to save to your photo library. It cannot see your photos.
  • Diagnostics: Bitz writes short technical notes (for example which pack was unlocked, or a step of a Nearby visit) to your device's system log. They contain no names. They stay on your device unless you share a device diagnostic report yourself.

Your save leaves your device only when you choose to: if you export a backup file (it contains your whole save, including friends' cat names and display names and today's step count; keep it private), share a picture or a milestone, or when your own device backup (iCloud or a computer) includes app data.

Nearby Friends (optional, off by default)

When you turn it on, and only while Bitz is open, Bitz uses Apple's encrypted Multipeer Connectivity to find other Bitz players physically nearby, over Wi-Fi or Bluetooth, without any server.

  • Before anyone acts: your phone announces itself to nearby devices with only a random 12-character code and the Bitz version. The code is new every time Nearby Friends starts. Each nearby phone that connects (it also has Nearby Friends on) receives your cat's look (coat, markings, eyes), what it wears and its growth stage, labelled with that code. This does not include your cat's name, your display name or your cat's permanent code. Your cat's look stays the same between sessions, so someone who has seen it before may recognise it.
  • When you invite someone (and confirm), that one player receives your cat's name, your display name and a permanent code that Bitz created for your cat, so they can see who is inviting and keep you as a friend. When you accept an invitation, the inviter receives the same about you. In a visit everyone agreed to, the cats in it see each other's names.
  • During a visit: actions such as waves and plays go to the cats in the visit.

Players you visit keep your cat's card (look, names and code) on their own device as a friend or a visit memory, just as you keep theirs. Bitz never shares your save, stars, purchases, Health data or location, and none of this reaches the developer.

Your controls: use a nickname as your display name (names other players could see are checked against a list of offensive words, on your device); turn Nearby Friends off at any time; remove a friend; Block a cat, so it is hidden and its invitations are declined; Report a player, which opens an email to us with their cat's name and display name that you can edit and choose to send.

Purchases (Apple)

Optional packs are bought from Apple through the App Store; Apple processes the payment and your Apple Account under Apple's Privacy Policy. Bitz reads Apple's signed record of which packs your Apple Account owns, on your device, to unlock them; Restore Purchases asks the App Store for that record again. The developer sees only the sales reports App Store Connect gives every developer, not who you are.

Information Apple shares with developers

If you agree in your device settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may give the developer crash reports and usage statistics about Bitz. Apple decides what these contain; Bitz adds nothing to them.

Support email and these web pages

Support email

  • What we receive: if you write to info@gibplay.com (including a Report email from Bitz), we receive your email address, the name your mail app sends with it, your message and anything you attach. A Report email also contains the other player's cat name and display name, as shown in the email before you send it.
  • What we use it for: only to answer you and to look into reports. We don't send newsletters or marketing email, and we don't add you to a mailing list.
  • How it travels: Cloudflare Email Routing receives mail for gibplay.com and forwards it to our Gmail mailbox, a Google account, where it is stored. Cloudflare states that it does not store message content; it records routing details (such as sender, recipient and subject), which we can see in its dashboard for a limited time (Cloudflare documents 31 days for its routing analytics). We write replies in Gmail, and they are sent through Brevo's email delivery service, so Brevo processes the reply, your email address and delivery records. Brevo keeps those delivery records for one month and is set not to store copies of our replies. Brevo adds open and click tracking to our replies, and Brevo has confirmed that it cannot be switched off for our account: links in our replies pass through Brevo's redirect service (r.info.gibplay.com) before reaching their destination, so Brevo receives the IP address and time of a click, and Brevo records when a reply is opened. Our account is set so that Brevo records these events without linking them to your email address. We don't ask anyone to agree to this tracking, and we don't use it for marketing or for anything else.
  • How long: we delete support conversations 12 months after the last message and Report emails 6 months after the report is resolved. We label support and Report emails in our mailbox and review it once a month, deleting messages that have reached these limits, so a message can remain up to about a month longer. If a specific message is still needed for an open issue, a dispute or a legal claim, we keep only that message, and only until the matter is closed. You can ask us at any time to delete your messages. We answer requests about your data, including deletion requests, within 30 days; if we have to keep something for one of these reasons, we tell you what and why.

These web pages

gibplay.com only presents GibPlay Studios and its games: it has no accounts, sign-ups, forms, newsletters, analytics, advertising or tracking. It is hosted on GitHub Pages and delivered through Cloudflare. To deliver and protect the pages, these providers receive your IP address, browser details and the page you request; GitHub logs visitor IP addresses for security. Cloudflare may also ask your browser to report pages that fail to load. The providers' dashboards may show us summary traffic statistics and security events, which can include IP addresses; we don't use them to identify visitors. The pages set no cookies and load no third-party fonts or scripts.

Providers and countries

The providers above are Cloudflare (email routing and website delivery), Google (Gmail), Brevo (sending our replies) and GitHub (website hosting), plus Apple for purchases and anything Apple shares with developers. We pass personal data to them only for these purposes, which means it is transferred abroad: they may process it outside your country, including in the United States. Brevo states that it hosts customer data in France and Belgium and that some of its own service providers are in the United States.

Purposes, legal bases and retention

The Turkish Personal Data Protection Law (KVKK, No. 6698) asks us to say why we process personal data, on which legal basis, how we collect it and how long we keep it. The Bitz app itself sends us nothing, so this covers support email and these web pages. We collect the data electronically: through the email you send us and, for the web pages, automatically through our hosting providers.

  • Answering questions about Bitz and your purchases (your email address, name, message and attachments): necessary for the performance of the licence agreement for Bitz between you and us (KVKK art. 5(2)(c)). Retention: see How long above.
  • Answering other messages and reviewing Report emails (as above, and for reports the reported player's cat name and display name): our legitimate interest in answering people who write to us and keeping Nearby Friends safe, without overriding your rights (KVKK art. 5(2)(f)). Retention: see How long above.
  • Delivering and protecting these web pages (IP address, browser details, the page requested, reports of failed page loads): our legitimate interest in running a working and secure website (KVKK art. 5(2)(f)). Kept by GitHub and Cloudflare under their own retention periods.
  • Legal claims and obligations: where needed to establish, exercise or defend a legal claim (KVKK art. 5(2)(e)) or to comply with a legal obligation (KVKK art. 5(2)(ç)), we keep the relevant messages until the matter is closed.

Your choices and rights

You can turn Health access off in Settings › Privacy & Security › Health › Bitz or in Bitz (Settings › Steps & Health), turn Nearby Friends off, and delete everything Bitz saved on your device by deleting the app (with its widgets). Deleting the app does not delete backup files you exported, your device backups, or the copies of your public card that other players' devices keep. Outside the app, the personal data we handle as the developer is support email: the messages and replies themselves, and the related delivery records at Cloudflare and Brevo.

You can ask us, at info@gibplay.com, whether we process your personal data and for what purpose, who we have passed it to (including abroad), to correct or delete it, and to tell those recipients about the correction or deletion; you can also object to a result that is to your detriment and arises only from automated analysis, and claim compensation for damage caused by unlawful processing (Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698, Article 11). To make a request, write to info@gibplay.com, preferably from the email address you used with us, so we can find your messages; Turkish law requires us to answer within 30 days. Where the EU General Data Protection Regulation applies to you, you also have the rights it gives you, such as access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and data portability. You may complain to your data protection authority (in Türkiye, the Personal Data Protection Authority, KVKK).

Children

Bitz is a general-audience game. The developer does not receive personal information from anyone through the app. Nearby Friends is off by default; if a child uses it, the names they type are shown to players they invite or whose invitation they accept, so a nickname is best. If you believe a child has emailed us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.

Bitz uygulaması bize kendiliğinden hiçbir veri göndermez; bu nedenle bu bölüm destek e-postalarını ve bu web sayfalarını kapsar.   • Bitz ve satın alımların hakkındaki sorulara yanıt vermek (e-posta adresin, adın, mesajın ve eklerin): seninle aramızdaki Bitz lisans sözleşmesinin ifası için gerekli olması (KVKK m. 5/2-c). Saklama süresi: yukarıdaki Ne kadar süre maddesine bak.

Uygulama dışında geliştirici olarak işlediğimiz kişisel veriler destek e-postalarıdır: mesajların ve yanıtların kendisi ile Cloudflare ve Brevo'daki ilgili gönderim kayıtları.

info@gibplay.com adresinden bize yazarak kişisel verilerinin işlenip işlenmediğini ve hangi amaçla işlendiğini, yurt içinde veya yurt dışında kimlere aktarıldığını öğrenebilir, düzeltilmesini veya silinmesini ve bu işlemlerin aktarıldığı kişilere bildirilmesini isteyebilirsin; yalnızca otomatik sistemlerle analiz edilmesi sonucunda aleyhine bir sonuç çıkmasına itiraz edebilir ve kanuna aykırı işleme nedeniyle zarara uğrarsan zararının giderilmesini talep edebilirsin (6698 sayılı Kişisel Verilerin Korunması Kanunu, madde 11). Talepte bulunmak için info@gibplay.com adresine, mesajlarını bulabilmemiz için tercihen bizimle yazıştığın e-posta adresinden yaz; Türk hukuku gereği en geç 30 gün içinde yanıt veririz. AB Genel Veri Koruma Tüzüğü senin için geçerliyse, erişim, düzeltme, silme, kısıtlama, itiraz ve veri taşınabilirliği gibi o Tüzüğün tanıdığı haklara da sahipsin. Veri koruma otoritesine (Türkiye'de Kişisel Verileri Koruma Kurumu, KVKK) şikâyette bulunabilirsin.

Çocuklar

Bitz genel kitleye yönelik bir oyundur. Geliştirici, uygulama aracılığıyla hiç kimseden kişisel bilgi almaz. Yakındaki Arkadaşlar varsayılan olarak kapalıdır; bir çocuk kullanırsa yazdığı adlar davet ettiği ya da davetini kabul ettiği oyunculara gösterilir, bu yüzden bir takma ad en iyisidir. Bir çocuğun bize kişisel bilgi içeren bir e-posta gönderdiğini düşünüyorsan bize yaz, sileriz.
