The companion

A little life that follows your day.

Bitz keeps your local time: stretching in the morning, roaming by day, settling in the evening and curling up to sleep at night. Come back and Bitz greets you.

Care

Fill the food and water bowls, brush them, give them a bath, clean the litter box and make the bed at night.

Play

Little games in every room, tricks to teach, toys to fetch and a lap to curl up on.

Personality

Curious, playful, sleepy or affectionate in their own mix. Bitz slowly learns to trust you and picks favourite toys and spots.

Growing up

From kitten to grown-up, one week at a time.

Your kitten becomes a young cat after a week and a grown-up after three. When Bitz is ready for the next stage, you choose the moment to see the change.

Growing up only changes how Bitz looks. Outfits, rooms and everything they've learned stay the same.

Rooms and seasons

Five rooms, four seasons, and a sky that follows the sun.

Bitz wanders between the main room, bedroom, kitchen, bathroom and garden. Winter brings snow and a wood stove, spring seedlings and butterflies, summer a fan and fireflies, autumn falling leaves and pumpkins. Earn stars by playing, spend them on furniture and colours, and arrange every room your way.

Room themes

Optional theme packs change a room's whole look, and every theme works in every room. Seasonal themes come back each year.

Roomy on iPad, too.

Bitz is made for iPhone and iPad, with the same rooms, seasons and care on both. The sky outside follows sunrise and sunset through the year, without asking for your location.

Widgets and the Charging Room

On your Home Screen, and by your bed.

Widgets

Small and medium Home Screen widgets, and Lock Screen widgets, show what Bitz is up to through the day. Pet or play right from a widget.

Widgets show a snapshot of Bitz that refreshes through the day, not a continuous animation. Open the app for the latest moment.

The Charging Room

With Bitz open, plug in your iPhone and turn it sideways: the Charging Room turns it into a cozy bedside clock with the time, the date, the battery level and Bitz carrying on with their evening. The screen stays on only while the Charging Room is showing and your iPhone is charging.

The Charging Room is a screen inside Bitz, not StandBy. For StandBy, add the Bitz widget there.

Nearby Friends

Visits when you're together.

Turn on Nearby Friends and invite a friend's cat over when you're in the same place. The cats meet in the garden for a little while; nothing else changes.

  • Off by default, and only works while Bitz is open on both phones.
  • Phones connect directly over Wi-Fi or Bluetooth with Apple's encrypted Multipeer Connectivity. No account, no server, no location.
  • A visit needs a yes on both phones. Players you meet can see your display name, so a nickname is best.
  • Block a cat in the Friends tab at any time. What Nearby Friends shares
